Before you upload

What makes a photo work

Most of the sculpting time goes into faces the camera half-hid. Four things make the difference.

face

Face the camera

Straight on, eyes open, nothing covering the face. A three-quarter angle works; a profile does not.

light_mode

Even light

Daylight near a window beats a flash. Hard shadows hide the shape of a nose and a chin.

crop_free

Get close

Head and shoulders filling the frame. A face twelve pixels wide in a group shot has nothing to work from.

checkroom

Show the outfit

If the jersey or the lab coat is the whole joke, make sure it is visible — or describe it in the notes.

Before you ask

Questions people actually ask

How many photos do you need?expand_more

One good one. If there is something the picture does not show — a haircut, a uniform, the exact shade of a football shirt — describe it in the notes and I will work from that.

Will it actually look like them?expand_more

It is a caricature, not a scan. The pop style is an oversized head on a simplified body, so expect 'that is definitely them' rather than a portrait. The preview render is exactly where you say the hair is wrong.

What is it made of?expand_more

PLA+, a rigid plant-based plastic, printed at 0.1mm layers and finished by hand. It is solid, light, and does not go brittle on a shelf.

Is it safe for kids to paint?expand_more

PLA takes ordinary water-based craft acrylics, which is what most kids' paint sets already are. The figure itself is a display piece, not a toy for a toddler — the keychain in particular is a small part, so it is not for under-threes.

How long does it take?expand_more

It depends on the queue and on how much sculpting the photo needs. You get a date in writing along with the preview render, before anything goes on the printer.

How does it reach me?expand_more

Tell me where you are in the notes and delivery is quoted with the preview. Collection in person can be arranged.

Can you do pets?expand_more

Yes. Dogs and cats translate into this style unreasonably well, and the same photo rules apply — face the camera, good light, close up.

Can I order a whole group?expand_more

A team, a wedding party, a classroom — it is done regularly. Put the headcount in the notes and send the photos after we have talked, and you will get a quote for the set.
