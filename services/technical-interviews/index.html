The cost

First rounds eat your senior engineers

Screening has the worst ratio in hiring: the most senior time spent per decision actually made, and most of those decisions are a no. It is also the easiest part of the loop to hand off, because it is the part with a repeatable bar.

  • trending_downAn hour of senior engineering time per candidate, before anyone has been advanced.
  • trending_downA bar that drifts, because a different engineer screens every week.
  • trending_downSlow scheduling, so strong candidates take another offer while they sit in your queue.

What you get

A scorecard, not a gut feel

Every screen comes back in the same shape, so candidates are comparable to each other rather than to whoever happened to interview them.
