The Problem

Where teams lose quality

layers_clear

Testing stops at the UI

Integrations and data flows ship unvalidated. The defects that reach production are not broken buttons — they are corrupted records, silent sync failures, and reports nobody can trust.

trending_up

Regression debt compounds

Every sprint adds cases to a manual pass nobody has time to run. Eventually the regression cycle is the release bottleneck, and coverage quietly shrinks to fit the calendar.

foundation

No QA foundation

Testing is improvised, ownership is unclear, and nothing is written down. The next hire starts from zero, and the same defects come back release after release.
